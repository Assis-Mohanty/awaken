AWAKEN PRIVACY POLICY

Effective 22 October 2026

AWAKEN ("the app") is developed by Assis Mohanty, Hyderabad, India. Contact: assis.mohanty.98@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Your hunter profile, quests, habits, Paths, progress and settings are stored only on your device. The app has no account system. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI Paths (optional)

If you turn on AI Paths, the app sends the following to our server, which forwards it to our AI provider, Cloudflare (Workers AI), to write your Path quests: your Path goal, current level and notes as you typed them; your chosen stats, active days and weekly time; your equipment and injury settings; and the titles, outcomes and "too easy / right / too hard" feedback of last week's Path quests. It does not send your name, health data, XP, level or any account information.

Requests are checked with Google Play Integrity and identified only by a random install ID. Our server does not store the content of requests or responses; it keeps a one-way hash of the install ID with request counts for 30 days to prevent abuse. Cloudflare states that Workers AI follows a zero data retention commitment: for standard inference requests, inputs and outputs are not retained after the response is delivered, and Cloudflare does not use them to train AI models. It does retain operational metadata such as timestamps, token counts and error codes, but not the content of requests or responses. (Source: Cloudflare Workers AI data usage documentation.)

Rewards (XP, gold, levels) are always calculated on your device by fixed rules, never by the AI. You can turn AI Paths off anytime in Settings; existing Paths stay on your device.

Reports

If you report a quest, its text and your chosen reason are sent and kept for up to 90 days to improve safety. Reports contain no identifier, so we cannot look up or delete a specific user's report.

Health Connect

If you choose to enable verified quests, the app reads Steps, Exercise sessions and Sleep sessions from Health Connect on your device to mark matching quests complete. This data is processed on-device only, is never uploaded (including to AI Paths), and is not used for advertising. You can revoke access anytime in Health Connect settings.

Use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Backups and deletion

Android may include app data in your Google account device backup according to your phone's backup settings. You can export or delete all data from Settings → Your data. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes.
