# AWAKEN

## Platform
android. Native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, phones, minSdk 28. Offline-first, no account.

## Users
Serious self-improvers, about 18 to 30, India and global English. They train, study, code and build discipline. They want a tool they respect. The game layer is a quiet bonus; anime is not part of the identity. Many have tried a habit app and quit within two weeks.

**Where it is used:**
- Morning, in bed or at a table, in soft light: reading the day's plan.
- Gym floor, in harsh light, one free hand, sweaty, glancing between sets.
- Desk, phone face up beside you while a focus session runs.

## What it does
Turns real effort into visible progress. A deterministic engine decides every reward; effort that can be checked (steps, workouts, sleep, focus sessions) counts for more than effort that is only claimed. Five stats, levels, ranks E to S, XP, gold and streaks sit on top of a plan for today, a focus tab with blockers and screen time, a gym tracker and a diet tracker.

## Position
Honest, private, hard but never cruel. Rewards are explainable. No sign-up. A missed day brings a comeback quest, never a punishment.

## Must never feel like
- A corporate dashboard.
- A children's game.
- A fitness bro app (black and neon, shouty).
- A soft wellness app (pastels, breathing-app sweetness).

## Constraints
- Engine rules, data and behaviour stay as they are. IA is approved in `design/IA.md`.
- Plain, second-person, sentence-case copy. Words are in `docs/DECLUTTER_PLAN.md`: quest, XP, rank, gold and Rank-up test stay; Gates, Vault, Hunter, System do not appear.
- Fonts must be bundleable and licensed for apps.
- WCAG AA, 48dp targets, reduced motion, dark and light both first class.
- Material 3 governs structure and controls; brand comes through theming, with no default Material look.
