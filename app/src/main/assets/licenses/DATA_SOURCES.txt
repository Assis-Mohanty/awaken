AWAKEN bundles these datasets, used offline. Nothing is downloaded.

Exercise library
  free-exercise-db by Yuri Honas and contributors - https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db
  Released under The Unlicense (public domain). Commit f00c92c7dcf1216a928a52c3706c7ce8e2f71ed5.

Food and nutrient data
  U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service, Beltsville Human Nutrition Research Center.
  FoodData Central. https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/ - Public domain (CC0 1.0).
  SR Legacy (April 2018) and Food and Nutrient Database for Dietary Studies (FNDDS 2021-2023, released 2024-10-31).

  Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0.
  Source: Public Health England, McCance and Widdowson's The Composition of Foods Integrated Dataset (CoFID) 2021.
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/composition-of-foods-integrated-dataset-cofid
  Licence: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ (see OGL_v3.txt).
  This app is not endorsed by the UK Government.

  Indian dishes marked "Estimate" are calculated by AWAKEN from its own recipes and the ingredient data above.
  Home cooking varies, so treat them as a guide. Values are for information only, not medical advice.

Strength benchmarks
  OpenPowerlifting - https://www.openpowerlifting.org - https://gitlab.com/openpowerlifting/opl-data
  Data released to the public domain (copyright waived). Used offline as percentile tables (bulk file 2026-11-07).
  This app uses data from the OpenPowerlifting project.

  US Army Fitness Test (AFT) Score Tables, effective 1 June 2025 - https://www.army.mil/e2/downloads/rv7/aft/AFT_Scoring_Scales_250601.pdf
  US Government work, public domain. Used for the plank and hand-release push-up level.
